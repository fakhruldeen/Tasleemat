## Tasleemat Forms Guide # Project Artifact: Procurement Management Plan **Document Reference:** `PMO-04.09.01` This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Procurement Management Plan** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework. --- ### 1. What? A governance artifact defining contract types, evaluation rules, legal jurisdiction, and vendor management protocols. --- ### 2. Why? Ensures legally sound, cost-effective acquisitions of external goods and services required for project delivery. --- ### 3. When? Developed during planning prior to issuing bid documents and enforced across all contracting activities. --- ### 4. Who? Authored by Procurement Manager and Project Manager, approved by Legal Counsel and Project Sponsor. --- ### Tailoring Tips * Utilize agile vendor contracting models (e.g., dynamic scope, fixed capacity) for iterative digital projects. * Enforce multi-stage international competitive bidding (ICB) for major capital equipment and infrastructure. --- ### Alignment & Dependencies #### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies) * **Mandatory:** * Project Scope Statement (PMO-04.02.05) * Cost Baseline (PMO-04.04.04) * Project Schedule (PMO-04.03.08) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Risk Register (PMO-04.08.02) * Resource Requirements (PMO-04.06.02) #### 2. Downstream Dependents * **Mandatory:** * Statement of Work / SOW (PMO-04.09.04) * RFP (PMO-04.09.05) * Contract Closure Report (PMO-07.02) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Vendor Performance Scorecard (PMO-06.09) * Contractor Status Report (PMO-06.03) --- ### 5. How? To accurately and professionally complete the **Procurement Management Plan**, the responsible party must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables): * **Procurement Strategy and Project Integration:** Integration strategy coordinating contract acquisitions with overall project schedule and scope baselines. * **Procurement Timetable and Milestone Alignment:** Key procurement lifecycle milestones (RFP issuance, bid evaluation, contract award, delivery). * **Procurement Roles and Sign-off Authority:** Defined roles (Procurement Manager, Legal Counsel, Technical Evaluators) and contracting authority limits. * **Vendor Performance Metrics and SLAs:** Objective metrics and service level agreements used to measure vendor performance. * **Standard Contract Types Selection:** Authorized contract structures (Firm Fixed Price, Time and Materials, Cost-Plus) mapped to deliverable types. * **Pre-Qualified Vendor Selection Approach:** Approach for evaluating and maintaining pre-qualified vendor lists and source directories. * **Legal Jurisdiction, Dispute Terms, and Currency:** Governing commercial legal jurisdiction, dispute resolution protocols, and billing currency. * **Procurement Risk and Independent Estimates:** Independent Cost Estimate (ICE) requirements and vendor default mitigation strategies. --- ### 6. Reference Example A fully completed, gold-standard reference example illustrating this artifact in practice is available: > 📖 **Completed Example:** [04_09_01_Procurement_Management_Plan_Example.md](../../../../../examples/en/04_Planning/09_Procurement/01_Procurement_Management_Plan/04_09_01_Procurement_Management_Plan_Example.md)
