## Tasleemat Forms Guide # Project Artifact: Stakeholder Engagement Plan **Document Reference:** `PMO-04.10.01` This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Stakeholder Engagement Plan** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework. --- ### 1. What? A governance artifact detailing engagement assessment matrices (C vs. D), inter-stakeholder dynamics, and targeted engagement protocols. --- ### 2. Why? Builds productive stakeholder partnerships, secures buy-in, resolves resistance, and sustains active project support. --- ### 3. When? Developed during planning after stakeholder identification and updated as stakeholder dynamics evolve. --- ### 4. Who? Authored by Project Manager and Change Lead, approved by Project Sponsor. --- ### Tailoring Tips * Integrate transparent sprint showcases and open backlog refinement for agile stakeholder collaboration. * Maintain highly confidential stakeholder management protocols for politically sensitive public transformations. --- ### Alignment & Dependencies #### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies) * **Mandatory:** * Stakeholder Register (PMO-03.04) * Stakeholder Analysis (PMO-03.05) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Project Charter (PMO-03.01) * Change Management Plan (PMO-04.01.02) #### 2. Downstream Dependents * **Mandatory:** * Project Status Reports (PMO-06.01) * Meeting Minutes (PMO-05.11) * **Optional / Contextual:** * OCM Strategy & Plan (PMO-04.11.01) * Issue Log (PMO-05.01) --- ### 5. How? To accurately and professionally complete the **Stakeholder Engagement Plan**, the responsible party must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables): * **Stakeholder Name and Role:** Designated key stakeholder, organizational title, and sphere of influence. * **Current vs Desired Engagement Level:** Assessment of current engagement (Unaware, Resistant, Neutral, Supportive, Leading) vs. desired target level. * **Engagement Gap Analysis:** Analytical evaluation explaining the gap between current and desired engagement states. * **Inter-Stakeholder Dynamics and Coalitions:** Analysis of political dynamics, alliances, and influence networks among stakeholder groups. * **Stakeholder Expectations and Key Concerns:** Documented primary expectations, operational concerns, and sensitivity triggers. * **Tailored Engagement Strategy:** Specific actionable interventions designed to move the stakeholder to the desired engagement state. * **Engagement Monitoring and Feedback Loop:** Process for evaluating engagement effectiveness and adjusting tactics throughout delivery. --- ### 6.
