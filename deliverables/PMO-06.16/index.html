Project Artifact: Risk and Issue Escalation Report

Document Reference:

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the Risk and Issue Escalation Report in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

1. What?

A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-06.16) establishing risk and issue escalation report protocols and governance baselines.

2. Why?

Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for risk and issue escalation report.

3. When?

Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

4. Who?

Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

Tailoring Tips

  • For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
  • For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

Alignment & Dependencies

1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)

  • Mandatory: Issue Log (PMO-05.01), Risk Register (PMO-04.08.02)
  • Optional / Contextual: Variance Analysis (PMO-06.04)

2. Downstream Dependents

  • Mandatory: Executive Steering Committee Dashboard (PMO-06.13), Decision Log (PMO-05.02)
  • Optional / Contextual: Change Request (PMO-05.03)

5. How?

To accurately and professionally complete the Risk and Issue Escalation Report, populate all required sections based on project context:

  • Escalation Trigger and Urgency Classification: Threshold breach triggering escalation (Budget +10%, Critical Path +14 days, Contractual dispute) and urgency rating.
  • Detailed Context of Risk or Issue: Factual, chronological narrative of the unresolved threat or materialized impediment.
  • Project Impact Analysis Schedule Cost Scope Reputation: Quantifiable financial, milestone delay, operational, and stakeholder reputation ramifications.
  • Evaluated Options and Recommended Course of Action: Alternative scenarios analyzed with pros, cons, costs, and the Project Manager recommended option.
  • Required Executive Intervention and Decision Deadline: Specific executive authority, budget release, or legal intervention required and hard deadline for decision.
