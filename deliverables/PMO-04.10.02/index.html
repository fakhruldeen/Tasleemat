## Tasleemat Forms Guide # Project Artifact: Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix **Document Reference:** This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework. --- ### 1. What? A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-04.10.02) establishing stakeholder engagement assessment matrix protocols and governance baselines. --- ### 2. Why? Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for stakeholder engagement assessment matrix. --- ### 3. When? Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions. --- ### 4. Who? Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees. --- ### Tailoring Tips * For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks. * For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs. --- ### Alignment & Dependencies #### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies) * **Mandatory:** Stakeholder Register (PMO-03.04), Stakeholder Analysis (PMO-03.05) * **Optional / Contextual:** Project Charter (PMO-03.01) #### 2. Downstream Dependents * **Mandatory:** Stakeholder Engagement Plan (PMO-04.10.01) * **Optional / Contextual:** Communications Distribution Matrix (PMO-04.07.02) --- ### 5. How? To accurately and professionally complete the **Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix**, populate all required sections based on project context: * **Stakeholder Identity and Project Role:** Name, organizational entity, and specific project touchpoint role of the stakeholder. * **Current versus Desired Engagement Level:** Assessment of current engagement (C) versus desired engagement (D): Unaware, Resistant, Neutral, Supportive, Leading. * **Engagement Gap and Risk Impact Analysis:** Strategic risks and schedule or governance exposure resulting from any delta between current and desired state. * **Targeted Engagement Strategy and Action Plan:** Specific relationship management actions, executive touchpoints, or incentives designed to close the gap. * **Monitoring Cadence and Feedback Indicators:** Schedule for re-assessing stakeholder sentiment and quantifiable indicators of active support. --- ### 6. Reference Example A fully completed, gold-standard reference example illustrating this artifact in practice is available: > 📖 **Completed Example:** [04_10_02_Stakeholder_Engagement_Assessment_Matrix_Example.md](../../../../../examples/en/04_Planning/10_Stakeholder/02_Stakeholder_Engagement_Assessment_Matrix/04_10_02_Stakeholder_Engagement_Assessment_Matrix_Example.md)
