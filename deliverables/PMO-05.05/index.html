## Tasleemat Forms Guide # Project Artifact: Quality Audit Report **Document Reference:** `PMO-05.05` This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Quality Audit Report** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework. --- ### 1. What? A formal audit report evaluating compliance with defined quality standards and process efficiency. --- ### 2. Why? Identifies process inefficiencies, non-conformance risks, and opportunities to enhance deliverable quality. --- ### 3. When? Executed at planned quality milestones during the execution phase. --- ### 4. Who? Conducted by an independent Quality Auditor or PMO Quality Specialist and delivered to the Project Manager. --- ### Tailoring Tips * Focus audits on critical deliverables and high-risk process steps. * Track non-conformance items until corrective actions are fully verified. --- ### Alignment & Dependencies #### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies) * **Mandatory:** * Project Management Plan (PMO-04.01.01) * Approved Baselines (Scope/Schedule/Cost) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Risk Register (PMO-04.08.02) * Stakeholder Engagement Plan (PMO-04.10.01) #### 2. Downstream Dependents * **Mandatory:** * Project Status Reports (PMO-06.01) * Change Log (PMO-05.04) * Lessons Learned Summary (PMO-07.01) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Variance Analysis (PMO-06.04) * Decision Log (PMO-05.02) --- ### 5. How? To accurately and professionally complete the **Quality Audit Report**, the responsible party must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables): * **Area Audited:** Specific processes, documentation, deliverables, or quality procedures evaluated. * **Good Practices from Similar Projects:** High-performing practices, techniques, and benchmarks identified during audit. * **Areas for Improvement:** Gaps, performance deviations, and recommended procedural optimizations. * **Deficiencies or Defects:** Recorded defects, non-compliance items, and assigned corrective actions. * **Implementation Tracking of Approved Changes:** Status of previously approved corrective actions, preventive actions, and defect repairs. * **Information to Share with Other Projects:** Valuable organizational insights, lessons, and quality benchmarks to disseminate. * **Comments:** General observations, auditor notes, and executive summary. --- ### 6.
