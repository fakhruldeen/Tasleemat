## Tasleemat Forms Guide # Project Artifact: Stakeholder Register **Document Reference:** `PMO-03.04` This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Stakeholder Register** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework. --- ### 1. What? A centralized register listing all stakeholders with their contact information, expectations, requirements, and classification. --- ### 2. Why? Effective stakeholder engagement is critical to managing expectations, securing alignment, and project success. --- ### 3. When? Created during project initiation and maintained continuously throughout the project lifecycle. --- ### 4. Who? Created and managed by the Project Manager with input from project sponsors and team members. --- ### Tailoring Tips * Categorize stakeholders by influence and interest to tailor communication strategies. * Regularly review contact details and role changes during phase transitions. --- ### Alignment & Dependencies #### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies) * **Mandatory:** * Project Charter (PMO-03.01) * Procurement Agreements * **Optional / Contextual:** * Program Charter (PMO-00.02) * Organizational Charts #### 2. Downstream Dependents * **Mandatory:** * Stakeholder Analysis (PMO-03.05) * Stakeholder Engagement Plan (PMO-04.10.01) * Communications Management Plan (PMO-04.07.01) * **Optional / Contextual:** * Change Management Strategy (PMO-04.11.01) * Team Charter (PMO-04.06.05) --- ### 5. How? To accurately and professionally complete the **Stakeholder Register**, the responsible party must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables): * **ID:** Unique identifier for the stakeholder (e.g., SH-01). * **Name:** Stakeholder’s name. If unknown, specify the position or organization. * **Position/Role:** The position or functional role the stakeholder holds in the organization or project. * **Contact information:** Communication channels for the stakeholder such as email address or phone number. * **Requirements:** High-level needs and critical requirements for the project and deliverables. * **Expectations:** Main expectations regarding project execution, outcomes, and governance. * **Classification:** Stakeholder categorization (e.g., internal/external, high/medium/low impact). --- ### 6. Reference Example A fully completed, gold-standard reference example illustrating this artifact in practice is available: > 📖 **Completed Example:** [03_04_Stakeholder_Register_Example.md](../../../examples/en/03_Initiating/03_04_Stakeholder_Register_Example.html)
