How to Complete FORM-03-01: Practical PMO Guidance

The Project Charter is not a technical specification—it is an executive agreement between the project sponsor and the PMO. Keep the total charter under 4 pages for Tier 1-2 projects, and under 8 pages for Tier 3 Enterprise efforts.

check_circle Mandatory Sponsoring: Never issue this form without formal sponsor signoff. Without executive endorsement, resource allocations cannot be enforced.
check_circle Assumption Log Cross-Check: Ensure all initial high-level risks identified here are immediately ported into FORM-03-02 (Assumption Log).
check_circle Bilingual Consistency: When submitting to government or audit committees in the GCC, use the official Arabic translation glossary provided in LEXICON.md.
