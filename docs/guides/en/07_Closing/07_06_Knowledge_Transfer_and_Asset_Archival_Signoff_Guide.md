# Project Artifact: Knowledge Transfer and Asset Archival Signoff

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Knowledge Transfer and Asset Archival Signoff** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-07.06) establishing knowledge transfer and asset archival signoff protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for knowledge transfer and asset archival signoff.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Transition to Operations Checklist (PMO-07.04), Training Plan and Log (PMO-04.11.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Lessons Learned Register (PMO-05.07)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Project or Phase Closeout (PMO-07.03)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Post-Implementation Review (PMO-07.05)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Knowledge Transfer and Asset Archival Signoff**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Knowledge Transfer Scope and Target Operations Team:** Designated operational entities, support teams, and technical domains covered by handover.
*   **Technical Documentation Repository and Architecture Assets:** Source code repositories, architecture diagrams, runbooks, and API documentation URLs archived.
*   **Operational Training and Shadowing Completion Record:** Completed hands-on workshops, admin shadowing sessions, and formal trainee attendance records.
*   **System Credentials Access Keys and License Transfers:** Secure handover of production administrative accounts, cloud root keys, certificates, and software licenses.
*   **Formal Operational Acceptance and Custody Signoff:** Operations Director and Project Manager joint authorization confirming complete custody transfer.

---
