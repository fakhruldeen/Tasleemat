# Project Artifact: Deliverable Technical Inspection Checklist

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Deliverable Technical Inspection Checklist** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-05.15) establishing deliverable technical inspection checklist protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for deliverable technical inspection checklist.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Quality Management Plan (PMO-04.05.01), Quality Metrics (PMO-04.05.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Work Breakdown Structure (PMO-04.02.06)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Product Acceptance Form (PMO-06.08), User Acceptance Testing Signoff (PMO-06.10)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Defect and Bug Tracking Log (PMO-06.15)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Deliverable Technical Inspection Checklist**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Deliverable Item and WBS Work Package:** Deliverable title, WBS code, and associated technical design specification reference.
*   **Technical Specification Compliance Verification:** Objective verification criteria evaluating functional and architectural conformity.
*   **Quality Criteria and Testing Protocol Results:** Automated unit test coverage, performance benchmark results, and physical inspection metrics.
*   **Observed Non Conformances and Defects:** Specific defects, punch list items, or documentation discrepancies requiring rectification.
*   **Technical Inspection Verdict and Formal Signoff:** Final technical recommendation: Approved for UAT, Approved with Minor punch-list, or Rejected.

---
