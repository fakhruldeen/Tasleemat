## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Action Items Log

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Action Items Log** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-05.13) establishing action items log protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for action items log.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Meeting Minutes (PMO-05.11), Issue Log (PMO-05.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Decision Log (PMO-05.02)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Project Status Report (PMO-06.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Team Member Status Report (PMO-06.02)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Action Items Log**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Action Item Identifier and Description:** Unique tracking ID and concise, actionable statement of the specific task or decision follow-up.
*   **Action Owner and Accountable Role:** Single designated individual with primary execution and delivery accountability.
*   **Target Completion Date and Priority:** Firm committed due date and priority weighting (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
*   **Current Execution Status and Notes:** Real-time status (Open, In Progress, Blocked, Complete) with recent progress notes.
*   **Resolution Evidence and Verification Signoff:** Deliverable link, test evidence, or Project Manager formal verification confirming closure.

---
