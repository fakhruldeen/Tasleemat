## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: PMO Maturity Assessment

**Document Reference:** `PMO-00.05`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **PMO Maturity Assessment** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **PMO Maturity Assessment**.
A formal strategic evaluation instrument measuring the capability, governance rigor, delivery consistency, and value enablement of a Project Management Office across five maturity levels.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured pmo maturity assessment, decisions risk ambiguity and lack objective backing.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed as required.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by key governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match project complexity and risk.
*   Ensure all key stakeholders are aligned before final sign-off.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Reporting Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Assessment Context

*   **Assessment Purpose and Scope:** What this PMO maturity assessment covers, the organizational boundaries evaluated, and the strategic improvement rationale behind the exercise.
*   **Assessment Lead and Date:** The designated assessor or governance review team accountable for the evaluation, and the baseline assessment completion date.
*   **Target Maturity Level:** The desired future maturity tier (Levels 1 to 5) targeted by the PMO across defined strategic planning horizons.

#### Dimension Scoring

*   **Governance and Strategy Alignment:** Scored capability rating and evidentiary findings regarding portfolio decision-making, strategic prioritization, and executive reporting alignment.
*   **Delivery Support and Methodology:** Scored capability rating and evidentiary findings regarding standard delivery framework adoption, lifecycle gates, and PM coaching support.
*   **People and Competency Development:** Scored capability rating and evidentiary findings regarding project management professional pathways, mentoring programs, and training curricula.
*   **Performance and Reporting:** Scored capability rating and evidentiary findings regarding milestone tracking, earned value visibility, variance logging, and KPI reporting.
*   **Tools and Technology Ecosystem:** Scored capability rating and evidentiary findings regarding PMIS tooling integration, workflow automation, and collaborative repository usage.

#### Improvement Roadmap

*   **Capability Gap Description:** The specific operational or structural gap separating current maturity performance from target capability requirements.
*   **Improvement Initiative:** The prioritized transformative action, process overhaul, or technology deployment planned to close the identified capability gap.
*   **Initiative Owner and Timeline:** The accountable individual responsible for delivery and the committed milestone completion date for the improvement initiative.
