# Project Artifact: OKR Alignment Matrix

**Document Reference:** `PMO-00.06`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **OKR Alignment Matrix** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework and PMI PMBOK standards.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **OKR Alignment Matrix**.
A formal strategic governance document connecting enterprise Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) directly with project deliverables to enable continuous value tracking and dynamic portfolio alignment.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured okr alignment matrix, strategic execution risks misalignment, lack of objective telemetry, and compromised governance.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed across iterative cycles.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by accountable governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match organizational complexity, regulatory standards, and delivery cadence.
*   Ensure all key cross-functional stakeholders are actively engaged throughout lifecycle reviews.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Performance Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Alignment Framework

*   **Strategic Cycle and Horizon:** The corporate planning cycle, fiscal year, and strategic review cadence governing the OKR alignment matrix.
*   **Enterprise Strategic Objective:** The overarching corporate priority or strategic theme this initiative directly advances.
*   **Portfolio Investment Category:** The investment classification (Strategic Growth, Operational Resilience, Transformative Innovation, or Mandatory Compliance).

#### OKR Mapping and Key Results

*   **Target Key Result ID and Metric:** The specific measurable Key Result identifier and target performance metric defined by executive leadership.
*   **Project Deliverable Contribution:** The tangible product capability, system feature, or project milestone directly contributing to Key Result fulfillment.
*   **Leading vs Lagging Indicator:** Classification whether the contribution measure provides immediate early progress signals (leading) or final outcome confirmation (lagging).

#### Confidence Tracking and Pivoting

*   **Quarterly Confidence Score:** Current subjective confidence rating (0.0 to 1.0) of achieving the Key Result given project execution trajectory.
*   **Confidence Variance Rationale:** Root-cause explanation for any score degradation or positive acceleration relative to the previous cycle baseline.
*   **Strategic Pivot Recommendation:** Mandated strategic intervention, scope adjustment, or resource reallocation if confidence drops below target threshold.
