## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Artifact: Product Acceptance Form

**Document Reference:** `PMO-06.08`

This document provides a detailed, professional reference for understanding the
purpose of the **Product Acceptance Form** and using it effectively as part of
the Tasleemat methodology.

---

### 1. What is it?
An official Tasleemat document called the **Product Acceptance Form**, used to
record the acceptance of each deliverable or component as it is validated. It
is part of process **5.5 Validate Scope** in the PMBOK Guide - Sixth Edition,
and should be done periodically throughout the project life cycle.

---

### 2. Why?
To ensure alignment with Tasleemat standards, to provide transparency, to
evidence that deliverables meet the agreed requirements, and to tie acceptance
to the requirements documentation and the requirements traceability matrix.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared and updated primarily during the **Monitoring and
Controlling Process Group** of the project lifecycle, as each deliverable is
validated and accepted.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the project manager or product owner, and
signed by the party accepting the product, including the client
representative where an external party is involved.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For a small project with only a few deliverables you may not need this
    form; acceptance may instead be recorded in the project status report.
*   You can add a column to indicate the verification method used to prove
    that the deliverables meet the requirements. In that case the form becomes
    a product verification, validation, and acceptance form.

### Alignment
*   Scope management plan
*   Requirements documentation
*   Requirements traceability matrix
*   Quality management plan

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **PRODUCT ACCEPTANCE FORM**, the
responsible party must populate the following critical sections based on the
project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project
variables):

*   **Product Acceptance Record:** For each requirement or deliverable
    accepted, record the ID, the requirement, the acceptance criteria, the
    validation method, the verification method if used, the status, the
    sign-off, the acceptance date, and any comments.
*   **Comments:** Add any comments relevant to the acceptance, such as
    conditions attached to it or items deferred.
