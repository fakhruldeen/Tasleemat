## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Project Health Check Matrix

**Document Reference:** `PMO-06.11`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Project Health Check Matrix** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Project Health Check Matrix**.
A structured diagnostic assurance instrument designed to perform an objective evaluation of project vitality across seven core dimensions to safeguard successful delivery.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured project health check matrix, decisions risk ambiguity and lack objective backing.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed as required.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by key governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match project complexity and risk.
*   Ensure all key stakeholders are aligned before final sign-off.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Reporting Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Review Context

*   **Review Purpose and Milestone Stage:** The objective of the independent health review and the project lifecycle phase or gate at which it is conducted.
*   **Lead Reviewer and Assessment Date:** The independent PMO auditor or assurance lead conducting the review and the inspection date.
*   **Overall Health Rating:** The synthesized overall health score (Red, Amber, or Green) representing aggregate delivery confidence.

#### Dimension Health Scores

*   **Governance and Sponsorship Health:** Health rating and findings on steering committee engagement, decision timeliness, and charter mandate.
*   **Scope and Change Control Health:** Health rating and findings on baseline stability, scope creep control, and change request rigor.
*   **Schedule and Milestone Health:** Health rating and findings on critical path adherence, milestone float, and delivery velocity.
*   **Cost and Budget Variance Health:** Health rating and findings on earned value metrics (CPI), cost variance, and EAC forecast credibility.
*   **Risk and Issue Exposure Health:** Health rating and findings on risk register currency, mitigation effectiveness, and unresolved issues.
*   **Stakeholder and Team Health:** Health rating and findings on team morale, resourcing levels, and stakeholder satisfaction.

#### Remediation Action Plan

*   **Critical Finding Description:** Key vulnerability or red-flag issue identified during the health check audit.
*   **Mandated Corrective Action:** Specific remedial intervention required to return the project domain to acceptable performance.
*   **Remediation Owner and Due Date:** The assigned individual accountable for corrective action execution and the strict due date.
