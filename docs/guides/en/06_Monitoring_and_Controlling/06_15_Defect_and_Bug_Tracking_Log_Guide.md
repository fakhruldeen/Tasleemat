# Project Artifact: Defect and Bug Tracking Log

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Defect and Bug Tracking Log** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-06.15) establishing defect and bug tracking log protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for defect and bug tracking log.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Deliverable Technical Inspection Checklist (PMO-05.15), Quality Metrics (PMO-04.05.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Quality Audit (PMO-05.05)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** User Acceptance Testing Signoff (PMO-06.10)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Product Acceptance Form (PMO-06.08)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Defect and Bug Tracking Log**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Defect Identifier and Title:** Unique defect ID, summary title, and affected component or module name.
*   **Severity Priority and Business Impact:** Severity rating (Blocker, Critical, Major, Minor) and SLA turnaround requirement.
*   **Steps to Reproduce and Observed versus Expected:** Detailed reproduction instructions, environment context, and expected versus actual result.
*   **Root Cause Diagnosis and Assigned Fixer:** Technical root cause classification (Code, Requirement, Environment) and assigned developer.
*   **Resolution Status and QA Retest Signoff:** Status (Open, In Fix, Retest, Verified Closed) and QA engineer verification signoff.

---
