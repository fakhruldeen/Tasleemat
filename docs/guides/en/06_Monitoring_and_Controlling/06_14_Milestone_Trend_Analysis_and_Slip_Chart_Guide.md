# Project Artifact: Milestone Trend Analysis and Slip Chart

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Milestone Trend Analysis and Slip Chart** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-06.14) establishing milestone trend analysis and slip chart protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for milestone trend analysis and slip chart.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Milestone List (PMO-04.03.04), Project Schedule (PMO-04.03.08)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Variance Analysis (PMO-06.04)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Project Status Report (PMO-06.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Change Request (PMO-05.03)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Milestone Trend Analysis and Slip Chart**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Milestone Identifier and Baseline Target Date:** Milestone code, deliverable name, and original approved baseline completion date.
*   **Historical Forecast Trend Data Points:** Bi-weekly or monthly sequential forecasts tracking milestone date movements over time.
*   **Current Variance and Slip Days Calculated:** Net difference in working days between current forecast and baseline date.
*   **Root Cause of Schedule Drift and Dependencies:** Underlying drivers (supplier delay, scope change, technical hurdles) causing milestone slippage.
*   **Schedule Recovery Actions and Target Stabilization:** Crashing, fast-tracking, or resource reallocations committed to recover milestone timing.

---
