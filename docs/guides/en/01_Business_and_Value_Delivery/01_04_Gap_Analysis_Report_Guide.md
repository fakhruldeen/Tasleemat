## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Gap Analysis Report

**Document Reference:** `PMO-01.04`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Gap Analysis Report** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Gap Analysis Report**.
A formal pre-project and value delivery document systematically comparing an organization's Current State (As-Is) with its Desired Target State (To-Be) to define capability discrepancies, risks, and bridge actions.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured gap analysis report, decisions risk ambiguity and lack objective backing.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed as required.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by key governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match project complexity and risk.
*   Ensure all key stakeholders are aligned before final sign-off.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Reporting Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Analysis Context

*   **Analysis Objective and Scope:** The strategic purpose and organizational business domain boundaries evaluated by this gap analysis.
*   **Current State Summary:** Executive summary of the baseline operating model, legacy systems, and existing operational capabilities.
*   **Desired Target State:** The envisioned future operating state, target business architecture, and required capability levels.

#### Gap Evaluation Matrix

*   **Functional and Process Gaps:** Specific discrepancies identified between current operational workflows and target process efficiency standards.
*   **Technical and Systems Gaps:** Deficiencies in current software infrastructure, data architecture, security tooling, and integration capabilities.
*   **People and Competency Gaps:** Human capital deficiencies, staffing shortages, and technical skill gaps required to sustain the target state.
*   **Risk and Business Impact:** The quantified operational risk, revenue exposure, and strategic impact of leaving identified gaps unaddressed.

#### Remediation Strategy

*   **Closure Initiative Description:** The specific project, enhancement sprint, or restructuring action designed to bridge the capability gap.
*   **Priority and Execution Feasibility:** The implementation urgency (Urgent/High/Medium/Low) and technical/financial execution feasibility rating.
*   **Assigned Owner and Target Date:** The accountable business lead and committed milestone target date for closing the capability gap.
