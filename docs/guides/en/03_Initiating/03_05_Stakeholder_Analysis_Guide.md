## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Stakeholder Analysis

**Document Reference:** `PMO-03.05`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Stakeholder Analysis** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
An analytical matrix mapping stakeholder power, interest, and sentiment to formulate targeted engagement approaches.

---

### 2. Why?
To identify potential conflicts, build alliances, and focus management attention on high-impact stakeholders.

---

### 3. When?
Conducted in the initiating phase and updated whenever stakeholder dynamics or project scopes change.

---

### 4. Who?
Performed by the Project Manager with input from the project team and sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Focus engagement plans on stakeholders with high power and high interest.
*   Re-evaluate attitudes regularly as project changes occur.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Stakeholder Register (PMO-03.04)
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Organizational Context & Culture Assessments

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Stakeholder Engagement Plan (PMO-04.10.01)
    *   Communications Management Plan (PMO-04.07.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   OCM Strategy & Plan (PMO-04.11.01)
    *   Training Plan (PMO-04.11.02)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Stakeholder Analysis**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **ID:** Unique identifier linking back to the Stakeholder Register (e.g., SH-01).
*   **Name or Role:** The name or role of the stakeholder being analyzed.
*   **Interest:** The level of interest the stakeholder has in project outcomes (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
*   **Influence:** The level of power or influence the stakeholder has over the project (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
*   **Attitude:** The stakeholder's stance towards the project (e.g., Champion, Positive, Neutral, Negative).

---
