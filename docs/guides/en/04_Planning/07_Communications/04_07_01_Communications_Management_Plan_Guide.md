## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Communications Management Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.07.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Communications Management Plan** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A governance artifact establishing communication channels, stakeholder matrices, distribution cadences, and glossaries.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures timely and appropriate generation and distribution of project information to sustain stakeholder alignment.

---

### 3. When?
Developed during initial planning and updated as stakeholder composition and governance needs change.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Project Manager and Communications Lead, approved by Sponsor and Key Stakeholders.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Establish automated chat channel webhooks and daily asynchronous standups for distributed agile teams.
*   Maintain formal contractual communications protocols and signed transmittal logs for public infrastructure projects.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Stakeholder Register (PMO-03.04)
    *   Stakeholder Analysis (PMO-03.05)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)
    *   Change Management Plan (PMO-04.01.02)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Status Reports (PMO-06.01)
    *   Meeting Minutes (PMO-05.11)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   OCM Strategy & Plan (PMO-04.11.01)
    *   Issue Log (PMO-05.01)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Communications Management Plan**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Stakeholder Group and Information Needs:** Identification of key stakeholder groups and their specific information, governance, and reporting requirements.
*   **Communication Purpose and Language:** The explicit intent of communication and designated working language for project artifacts.
*   **Approved Communication Channels and Tools:** Designated platforms (email, intranet, project portal, status dashboards) used for distribution.
*   **Confidentiality and Information Security Constraints:** Access control guidelines, NDAs, and security protocols governing sensitive project data.
*   **Communication Item and Target Audience:** Specific report or meeting artifact (e.g., Weekly Status Report, Executive Steering Deck) and recipients.
*   **Frequency, Format, and Responsible Sender:** Distribution cadence, file format (PDF, dashboard), and assigned sender responsible for delivery.
*   **Urgent Escalation Process:** Step-by-step protocol for escalating critical issues, budget alerts, and schedule delays to executive leadership.
*   **Project Terminology and Acronym Glossary:** Standard glossary defining technical acronyms, specialized terms, and abbreviations used across project documents.
