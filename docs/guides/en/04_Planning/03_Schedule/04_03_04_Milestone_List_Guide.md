## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Milestone List

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.03.04`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Milestone List** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A consolidated schedule artifact identifying milestone IDs, descriptions, target dates, type, and verification criteria.

---

### 2. Why?
Enables executive progress monitoring, aligns stakeholder delivery expectations, and tracks major governance gates.

---

### 3. When?
Defined during initial schedule planning and tracked throughout project execution.

---

### 4. Who?
Created by Project Manager and Scheduler, approved by Project Sponsor and Client.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Track release completion and demo showcase events for agile product iterations.
*   Link contractual milestones directly to payment release gates for fixed-price contracts.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Work Breakdown Structure / WBS (PMO-04.02.06)
    *   Scope Statement (PMO-04.02.05)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Resource Requirements (PMO-04.06.02)
    *   Risk Register (PMO-04.08.02)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Schedule Baseline (PMO-04.03.08)
    *   Earned Value Analysis / EVA (PMO-06.05)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Release Plan (PMO-04.03.09)
    *   Lookahead Planning Log (PMO-04.03.10)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Milestone List**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Contractual and Regulatory Milestones:** Legally binding delivery milestones, compliance deadlines, and contractual gate events.
*   **Target Dates and Deliverables (Mandatory):** Fixed target calendar dates and verified deliverables associated with mandatory milestones.
*   **Internal Project Gate Milestones:** Major internal transition points, architectural baseline completions, and integration checkpoints.
*   **Target Dates and Deliverables (Internal):** Planned schedule dates and required internal outcomes for managerial review milestones.
*   **Verification Evidence and Sign-off Lead:** Required audit evidence, formal artifacts, and designated approval authority for milestone achievement.
*   **Milestone Critical Path Impact:** Assessment of whether the milestone resides on the critical path and associated delivery float.
