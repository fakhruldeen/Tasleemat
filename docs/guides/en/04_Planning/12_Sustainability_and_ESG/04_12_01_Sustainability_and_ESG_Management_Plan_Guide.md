## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Sustainability and ESG Management Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.12.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Sustainability and ESG Management Plan** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework and PMI PMBOK standards.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Sustainability and ESG Management Plan**.
A formal project planning baseline defining measurable environmental, social, and governance (ESG) targets, carbon reduction strategies, circular resource utilization, and ethical compliance standards.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured sustainability and esg management plan, strategic execution risks misalignment, lack of objective telemetry, and compromised governance.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed across iterative cycles.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by accountable governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match organizational complexity, regulatory standards, and delivery cadence.
*   Ensure all key cross-functional stakeholders are actively engaged throughout lifecycle reviews.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Performance Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Sustainability Mandate and Scope

*   **Sustainability Vision and Regulatory Baseline:** The organizational ESG mandate, environmental regulatory frameworks, and statutory compliance standards governing the project.
*   **Carbon Footprint Reduction Target:** Quantified greenhouse gas (GHG) emission reduction targets spanning direct execution (Scope 1/2) and supply chain operations (Scope 3).
*   **Circular Economy and Material Strategy:** Policies governing zero-waste design, sustainable material selection, energy efficiency, and asset recycling / reuse pathways.

#### Social and Ethical Governance

*   **Social Impact and Community Engagement:** Measures ensuring positive community impact, workforce diversity, labor safety standards, and local stakeholder benefits.
*   **Ethical Procurement and Supplier ESG Standards:** Mandatory vendor qualification criteria regarding human rights, fair labor practices, and sustainable supply chain transparency.
*   **ESG Risk and Compliance Monitoring:** Specific environmental and social risk exposures, mitigation plans, and regulatory inspection cadences.

#### ESG Metrics and Decommissioning

*   **ESG Key Performance Indicators:** Measurable scorecard tracking energy consumption, carbon offsets, diversity ratios, and ethical audit scores across sprints.
*   **Sustainable Decommissioning and Handover:** Procedures ensuring environmentally responsible asset disposal, data purging, and ecological post-project decommissioning.
*   **Sustainability Audit Lead and Review Cadence:** The designated sustainability officer accountable for compliance audits and the periodic ESG review schedule.
