## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Cost Baseline

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.04.04`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Cost Baseline** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A time-phased budget model representing cumulative planned value (PV), funding limits, and control account allocations.

---

### 2. Why?
Serves as the authorized benchmark against which actual cost performance and earned value are measured.

---

### 3. When?
Formally baselined at the close of planning and maintained under strict change control.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Project Manager and Cost Controller, approved by Project Sponsor and Steering Committee.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Represent as periodic release budget allocations for agile deliveries.
*   Incorporate strict monthly cash flow draw-down milestones for major capital investments.

---

*   **Mandatory:**

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**

    *   Variance Analysis (PMO-06.04)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Cost Estimating Worksheet (PMO-04.04.03)
    *   Procurement Budget Plan (PMO-04.09.01)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Cost Baseline**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Reporting Period and Planned Expenditures:** Periodic time-phased planned expenditures across months or fiscal quarters.
*   **Cumulative Planned Value (S-Curve PV):** The authorized cumulative planned budget curve from project start to completion.
*   **Budget at Completion (BAC) Total:** The total authorized baseline budget excluding management reserves.
*   **Control Account and Work Package Allocations:** Approved baseline budget figures mapped to specific WBS control accounts.
*   **Contingency Reserve Distribution:** Distribution of project contingency reserves assigned across control accounts.
*   **Periodic Funding Requirements:** Total cash outlay required per period, incorporating funding step increments.
*   **Management Reserve and Total Project Budget:** Executive management reserve amount and the total authorized project budget (BAC + Management Reserve).
