## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Team Psychological Safety and Wellbeing Index

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.06.06`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Team Psychological Safety and Wellbeing Index** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework and PMI PMBOK standards.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Team Psychological Safety and Wellbeing Index**.
A formal human-centered leadership diagnostic tool measuring team psychological safety, cognitive load distribution, and collaborative well-being to sustain high performance.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured team psychological safety and wellbeing index, strategic execution risks misalignment, lack of objective telemetry, and compromised governance.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed across iterative cycles.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by accountable governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match organizational complexity, regulatory standards, and delivery cadence.
*   Ensure all key cross-functional stakeholders are actively engaged throughout lifecycle reviews.

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Assessment Baseline

*   **Assessment Period and Cadence:** The evaluation sprint, sprint cadence, and designated survey period for measuring team psychological safety and cognitive well-being.
*   **Facilitator and Working Model:** The designated agile coach, scrum master, or people lead facilitating the assessment, and the team's working arrangement (Co-located, Hybrid, or Fully Remote).
*   **Team Response Participation Rate:** The percentage of team members participating in the anonymous assessment to validate sample representation.

#### Psychological Safety Dimensions

*   **Vulnerability and Open Inquiry Score:** Aggregated rating reflecting team members' comfort in asking questions, admitting mistakes, and seeking help without fear of penalty.
*   **Constructive Dissent and Innovation Score:** Aggregated rating reflecting psychological comfort in challenging assumptions, proposing novel ideas, and offering dissenting views.
*   **Blameless Culture and Mutual Support Score:** Aggregated rating reflecting perceived team trust, peer collaboration, and constructive root-cause problem solving.

#### Cognitive Load and Well-being

*   **Cognitive Load and Overtime Health:** Quantitative assessment of sustained work pressure, cognitive context-switching frequency, and overtime hours worked.
*   **Focus Time and Meeting Load Ratio:** Ratio of uninterrupted deep-work focus time compared to total hours spent in synchronous meetings and ceremonies.
*   **Well-being Action and Working Agreement Update:** Agreed interventions, meeting hygiene rules, focus-block policies, or team charter amendments enacted to protect team vitality.
