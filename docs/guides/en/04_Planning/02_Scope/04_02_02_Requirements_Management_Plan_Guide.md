## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Requirements Management Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.02.02`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Requirements Management Plan** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A governance guide detailing processes and tools used across the requirements engineering lifecycle.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures clear alignment between stakeholder business needs and delivered technical solutions.

---

### 3. When?
Authored early during planning and applied continuously as requirements are refined.

---

### 4. Who?
Created by Lead Business Analyst or Project Manager, approved by Project Sponsor and Technical Lead.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Utilize agile user story backlog refinement techniques for iterative delivery models.
*   Apply formal traceability matrices for safety-critical and regulated product development.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)
    *   Stakeholder Requirements
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Product Vision (PMO-03.02)
    *   Assumption Log (PMO-03.03)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Work Breakdown Structure / WBS (PMO-04.02.06)
    *   Project Schedule (PMO-04.03.08)
    *   Cost Estimates (PMO-04.04.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Product Backlog (PMO-04.02.08)
    *   Quality Metrics (PMO-04.05.02)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Requirements Management Plan**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Requirements Elicitation Techniques:** Methods used to gather stakeholder needs including interviews, focus groups, and surveys.
*   **Requirements Analysis and Elaboration:** Analytical processes applied to structure, clarify, and model requirements.
*   **Stakeholder Engagement for Requirements:** Strategy for maintaining active stakeholder participation throughout requirements definition.
*   **Requirements Taxonomy and Categories:** Classification scheme covering business, stakeholder, solution, functional, and non-functional requirements.
*   **Requirements Documentation Standards:** Formatting guidelines and documentation artifacts used to record verified requirements.
*   **Requirements Prioritization Scheme:** Ranking methodology (such as MoSCoW, weighted scoring, or value ranking) to prioritize scope.
*   **Requirements Product Metrics:** Quantitative metrics measuring requirements volatility, defect density, and coverage.
*   **Traceability Structure and Attributes:** Attributes and linkages tracked across the requirements lifecycle to ensure bidirectional traceability.
*   **Requirements Configuration Control:** Procedures for baseline versioning and evaluating changes to documented requirements.
*   **Requirements Verification Method:** Techniques ensuring requirements are complete, consistent, unambiguous, and testable.
*   **Validation and User Acceptance Strategy:** Approach for validating that final deliverables fulfill underlying business objectives.
