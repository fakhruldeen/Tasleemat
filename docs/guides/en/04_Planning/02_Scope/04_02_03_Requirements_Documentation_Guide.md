## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Requirements Documentation

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.02.03`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Requirements Documentation** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A comprehensive specification detailing business, stakeholder, functional, non-functional, and transition requirements.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides an unambiguous contractual and technical baseline for design, development, and testing.

---

### 3. When?
Elicited during initial planning and baseline sign-off, updated via formal change control.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Business Analyst and Product Owner, verified by Lead Architect and Approved by Sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Structure as hierarchical user stories and epics with acceptance criteria for agile execution.
*   Utilize formal IEEE/ISO specifications for regulated enterprise software engineering.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)
    *   Stakeholder Requirements
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Product Vision (PMO-03.02)
    *   Assumption Log (PMO-03.03)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Work Breakdown Structure / WBS (PMO-04.02.06)
    *   Project Schedule (PMO-04.03.08)
    *   Cost Estimates (PMO-04.04.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Product Backlog (PMO-04.02.08)
    *   Quality Metrics (PMO-04.05.02)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Requirements Documentation**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Business Objectives and Goals:** High-level organizational motivations, strategic drivers, and target business problems to solve.
*   **Business Process Impact:** Current-state vs. target-state operational workflow enhancements and process impacts.
*   **Business Value and Success Criteria:** Measurable commercial, operational, or customer value expected upon delivery.
*   **User Persona and Needs Summary:** Detailed operational requirements categorized by specific stakeholder groups and user personas.
*   **Stakeholder Pain Points and Expectations:** Critical operational pain points addressed and expected behavioral outcomes.
*   **Functional Requirements Specification:** Detailed capabilities, system interactions, features, and functional behavior.
*   **Technical Architecture and System Constraints:** System interfaces, infrastructure prerequisites, data architecture, and technological boundaries.
*   **Non-Functional Requirements (Quality of Service):** Performance thresholds, security standards, availability, scalability, and usability criteria.
*   **Transition and Readiness Requirements:** Data migration, end-user training, operational handover, and rollout support prerequisites.
