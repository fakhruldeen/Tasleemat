# Project Artifact: Probability and Impact Matrix

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.08.04`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Probability and Impact Matrix** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A calibrated 5x5 matrix establishing risk exposure thresholds, color-coded priority zones, and escalation rules.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides objective visual categorization of risks to guide appropriate management responses and resource allocations.

---

### 3. When?
Established during risk planning and used continuously during qualitative risk assessments.

---

### 4. Who?
Defined by Risk Manager and Project Manager, approved by Project Sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Calibrate grid thresholds to match organizational risk appetite and tolerance levels.
*   Incorporate dual threat/opportunity scoring grids for commercial and value-driven projects.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)
    *   Assumption Log (PMO-03.03)
    *   Project Baselines (Scope/Schedule/Cost)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   AI Governance Plan (PMO-02.02)
    *   Procurement Strategy (PMO-04.09.02)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Risk Register (PMO-04.08.02)
    *   Risk Reports (PMO-04.08.06)
    *   Risk Audits (PMO-06.06)
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Risk Mitigation Action Plan (PMO-04.08.07)
    *   Decision Log (PMO-05.02)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Probability and Impact Matrix**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Matrix Grid Dimensions and Scaling:** Description of the matrix layout (e.g., 5x5 grid) and mathematical scoring formula.
*   **Risk Tolerance and Severity Bands:** Definition of risk severity bands: High (Red), Medium (Yellow), and Low (Green).
*   **Threat Evaluation Grid Mapping:** The calibrated scoring values and color-coded zones for negative risk threats.
*   **Opportunity Evaluation Grid Mapping:** The calibrated scoring values and color-coded zones for positive project opportunities.
*   **Mandatory Treatment Thresholds:** Thresholds requiring mandatory immediate mitigation and dedicated contingency funding.
*   **Executive Escalation Protocols:** Protocols governing when high-severity risks must be escalated to the Executive Sponsor or PMO.
