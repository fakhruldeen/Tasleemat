Honest Boundaries & Scope

Understand exactly what Tasleemat provides and what it does not do.

✅ What Tasleemat Provides

  • 114 Canonical Deliverables: Reusable markdown templates in English and Arabic so teams never start from blank sheets.
  • Practitioner Guidance & Examples: Step-by-step authoring guides, completed realistic examples, and bilingual glossaries.
  • Governance Sizing & Tailoring: Structured tailoring rules across 3 Sizing Tiers and 5 Delivery Archetype Packs.
  • Machine-Readable Contracts: 114 companion JSON Schemas (Draft 2020-12) for automated validation.
  • Developer & AI Assistant Integration: Native Python SDK, CLI (tasleemat), and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server.

❌ What Tasleemat Does Not Do

  • Not a Live PMO Application: Does not host or execute project tracking workflows, dashboards, or scheduling engines.
  • Not an Approval Authority: Does not replace project steering committees, sponsors, or governance gates.
  • Not a Certification Authority: Does not issue compliance seals, audits, or legal certifications.
  • Not an Autonomous Project Agent: Does not autonomously make executive or contractual decisions.
  • Not a Shared Database: Does not store your confidential project deliverables or sensitive operational data.
