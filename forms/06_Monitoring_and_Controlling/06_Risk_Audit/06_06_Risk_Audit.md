# RISK AUDIT REPORT - LLM GENERATION GUIDE

> **System Prompt / Instructions:**
> This document serves as the detailed instruction set for generating the `RISK AUDIT REPORT`. When asked to populate this form, use the guidance provided for each section below to accurately generate the required content. Reference `parameters.md` for global project variables.

> **Context & Definition:**
> Risk audits are used to evaluate the effectiveness of the risk identification, risk responses, and risk management process as a whole. Information reviewed in a risk audit can include risk event audits (risk events, causes, responses), risk response audits (risk event, responses, success, actions for improvement), risk management processes (process, compliance, tools and techniques used), good practices, and areas for improvement. The risk audit is a tool used in process 11.7 Control Risks in the PMBOK® Guide – Sixth Edition, and is conducted periodically as needed.

> **Alignment:**
> Risk management plan, Risk register, Risk report

---

### Audit Information
**Instruction:** Record the audit scope, period covered, level of analysis, the reference documents reviewed (risk management plan, risk register, risk report), and the audit method.

---

### Risk Event Audits
**Instruction:** For each risk event reviewed, list the event from the risk register, identify the root cause from the risk register, describe the response implemented, and discuss in the comment if there was any way to have foreseen the event and respond to it more effectively.

---

### Risk Response Audits
**Instruction:** For each risk response reviewed, list the risk event and the risk response from the risk register, indicate whether the response was successful, and identify any opportunities for improvement in the risk response.

---

### Risk Management Process Audit
**Instruction:** Assess the risk management processes in two parts. First, for process compliance, indicate whether Plan Risk Management, Identify Risks, Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis, Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis, Plan Risk Responses, and Control Risks were followed as indicated in the risk management plan. Second, for tools and techniques, identify the tools and techniques used in the various risk management processes and whether they were successful.

---

### Good Practices
**Instruction:** Describe any practices that should be shared for use on other projects. Include any recommendations to update and improve risk forms, templates, policies, procedures, or processes to ensure these practices are repeatable.

---

### Areas for Improvement
**Instruction:** Describe any practices that need improvement, the improvement plan, and any follow-up dates or information for corrective action.

---

### Comments
**Instruction:** Document any comments that add relevance to this report.
