## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Risk Audit

**Document Reference:** `PMO-06.06`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Risk Audit** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A structured audit report assessing compliance with risk processes and analyzing the efficacy of risk response strategies.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures continuous improvement in risk identification and verifies that organizational risk policies are followed.

---

### 3. When?
Conducted periodically at designated milestones or upon major risk triggers during project execution.

---

### 4. Who?
Conducted by an independent Risk Auditor or PMO Lead and submitted to the Project Manager and Governance Board.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale audit depth based on project risk classification and regulatory compliance requirements.
*   Focus on recurring operational risk patterns in iterative sprint retrospectives.

---

### Alignment
Integrates with Risk Management Plan, Risk Register, Risk Report, and Quality Audit protocols.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Risk Audit**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Audit Scope:** The defined scope and boundaries of the risk audit.
*   **Period Covered:** The timeframe examined by the risk audit.
*   **Level of Analysis:** The organizational or WBS level examined during the audit.
*   **Reference Documents Reviewed:** Key governance documents and logs reviewed during the audit.
*   **Audit Method:** The methodology and evaluation techniques applied during the audit.
*   **Risk Event Audits:** Evaluation of materialized risk events, root causes, response effectiveness, and foreseeability.
*   **Risk Response Audits:** Detailed assessment of planned risk responses and their practical execution success.
*   **Process Compliance:** Evaluation of organizational compliance with established PMBOK risk processes.
*   **Tools and Techniques:** Assessment of the effectiveness of risk assessment tools and analytical models applied.
*   **Good Practices:** Proven risk management practices and techniques recommended for broader adoption.
*   **Areas for Improvement:** Identified procedural deficiencies, corrective action plans, owners, and due dates.
*   **Comments:** Additional audit observations, limitations, or stakeholder commentary.
