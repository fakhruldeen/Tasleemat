# Project Artifact: Bottom-Up Estimating Worksheet

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.04.03.B`

This document provides a comprehensive reference to understand the purpose and usage of the **Bottom-Up Estimating Worksheet**.

---

### 1. What?
A highly detailed cost estimating worksheet that breaks down the costs associated with an individual work package or activity into specific resource categories (labor, materials, supplies, equipment, travel, indirect costs, and reserves).

### 2. Why?
To provide the most accurate estimate possible by calculating costs at the lowest level of the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and aggregating them upwards. It prevents broad assumptions by forcing estimators to consider every single cost driver.

### 3. When?
Developed during the **PLANNING Process Group** (Process 7.2 Estimate Costs) when detailed information (such as technical requirements, engineering drawings, or vendor quotes) becomes available.

---

### Alignment
The bottom-up cost estimating worksheet should be aligned and consistent with the following documents:
• Cost management plan
• Scope baseline
• Project schedule
• Quality management plan
• Resource requirements
• Risk register
• Lessons learned register
