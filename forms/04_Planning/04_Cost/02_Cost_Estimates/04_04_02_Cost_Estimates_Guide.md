## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Cost Estimates

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.04.02`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Cost Estimates** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A structured artifact tabulating labor, materials, equipment, and contingency reserve costs across all work packages.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides the definitive cost projections necessary to secure funding and establish the project budget baseline.

---

### 3. When?
Developed during planning after activity definition and duration estimation, prior to final budget sign-off.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Project Cost Estimator and Technical Leads, reviewed and consolidated by Project Manager.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Use team blended rates and story points for agile team capacity cost projections.
*   Detail discrete bottom-up unit rate analyses for major construction and procurement contracts.

---

### Alignment
Feeds directly into Cost Baseline, Cost Estimating Worksheet, Procurement Budget, and Risk Contingency planning.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Cost Estimates**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Activity Identifier and Description:** Unique activity code and concise work summary for each estimated item.
*   **Direct Labor Cost Estimate:** Estimated direct staffing expenses calculated from effort hours and labor rates.
*   **Materials and Equipment Cost Estimate:** Estimated costs for hardware, raw materials, software licensing, and equipment.
*   **Total Base Cost Estimate:** Consolidated base financial estimate prior to reserve calculations.
*   **Labor Rate Categories and Rate Cards:** Standard billing rate categories, consultant day-rates, and internal unit costs.
*   **Historical Data and Estimating Assumptions:** Benchmark data, vendor quotation references, and key assumptions applied.
*   **Contingency Reserve Allocation:** Calculated risk contingency reserve allocated for identified known-unknown risks.
*   **Total Activity Cost with Contingency:** Final baseline cost figure inclusive of contingency reserves for budgeting.
