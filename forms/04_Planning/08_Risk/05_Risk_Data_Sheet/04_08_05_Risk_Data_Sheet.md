# RISK DATA SHEET - LLM GENERATION GUIDE

> **System Prompt / Instructions:**
> This document serves as the detailed instruction set for generating the `RISK DATA SHEET`. When asked to populate this form, use the guidance provided for each section below to accurately generate the required content. Reference `parameters.md` for global project variables.

---

### Risk ID
**Instruction:** Enter a unique risk identifier.

---

### Risk description
**Instruction:** Provide a detailed description of the risk.

---

### Status
**Instruction:** Enter the status as open or closed.

---

### Risk cause
**Instruction:** Describe the circumstances or drivers that are the source of the risk.

---

### Probability
**Instruction:** Determine the likelihood of the event or condition occurring.

---

### Impact
**Instruction:** Describe the impact on one or more of the project objectives.

---

### Score
**Instruction:** If you are using numeric scoring, multiply the probability times the impact to determine the risk score. If you are using relative scoring then combine the two scores (e.g., highlow or medium-high).

---

### Reponses
**Instruction:** Describe the planned response strategy to the risk or condition.

---

### Revised probability
**Instruction:** Determine the likelihood of the event or condition occurring after the response has been implemented.

---

### Revised impact
**Instruction:** Describe the impact once the response has been implemented.

---

### Revised score
**Instruction:** Enter the revised risk score once the response has been implemented.

---

### Responsible party
**Instruction:** Identify the person responsible for managing the risk.

---

### Actions
**Instruction:** Describe any actions that need to be taken to respond to the risk.

---

### Secondary risks
**Instruction:** Describe new risks that arise out of the response strategies taken to address the risk.

---

### Residual risk
**Instruction:** Describe the remaining risk after response strategies.

---

### Contingency plan
**Instruction:** Develop a plan that will be initiated if specific events occur, such as missing an intermediate milestone. Contingency plans are used when the risk or residual risk is accepted.

---

### Contingency funds
**Instruction:** Determine the funds needed to protect the budget from overrun.

---

### Contingency time
**Instruction:** Determine the time needed to protect the schedule from overrun.

---

### Fallback plans
**Instruction:** Devise a plan to use if other response strategies fail.

---

### Comments
**Instruction:** Provide any comments or additional helpful information about the risk event or condition.
