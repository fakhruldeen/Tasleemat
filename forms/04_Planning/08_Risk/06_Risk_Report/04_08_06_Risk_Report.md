### Executive summary
**Instruction:** A statement describing the overall project risk exposure and major individual risks affecting the project, along with the proposed responses for trends.

---

### Overall project risk
**Instruction:** Provide a description of the overall risk of the project, including: • High-level statement of trends • Significant drivers of overall risk • Recommended responses to overall risk

---

### Individual project risks
**Instruction:** Analyze and summarize information associated with individual project risks, including: • Number of risks in each box of the probability impact matrix • Key metrics • Active risks • Newly closed risks • Risks distribution by category, objective, and score • Most-critical risks and changes since last report • Recommended responses to top risks

---

### Quantitative analysis
**Instruction:** Summarize the results of quantitative risk analysis, including: • Results from quantitative assessments (S-curve, tornado, etc.) • Probability of meeting key project objectives • Drivers of cost and schedule outcomes • Proposed responses

---

### Reserve status
**Instruction:** Describe the reserve status, such as reserve used, reserve remaining, and an assessment of the adequacy of the reserve.

---

### Risk audit results (if applicable)
**Instruction:** Summarize the results of a risk audit of the risk management processes.
