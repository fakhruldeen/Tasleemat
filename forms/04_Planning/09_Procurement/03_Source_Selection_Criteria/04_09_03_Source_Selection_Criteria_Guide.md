## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Source Selection Criteria

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.09.03`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Source Selection Criteria** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A structured scoring model defining weighted evaluation criteria, scoring scales, candidate rating matrices, and award recommendations.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures transparent, objective, and legally defensible vendor selection based on best overall value.

---

### 3. When?
Established prior to RFP issuance and utilized during proposal evaluation and vendor selection.

---

### 4. Who?
Created by Procurement Evaluation Committee and Project Manager, approved by Procurement Director and Sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Emphasize practical technical coding challenges and team cultural fit for agile software development.
*   Apply strict mandatory compliance thresholds and lowest evaluated compliant price criteria for standard commoditized purchases.

---

### Alignment
Included within Request for Proposal (RFP) packages and directly drives Tender Evaluation Reports and Contract Awards.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Source Selection Criteria**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Technical Capability and Understanding (Weight):** Scoring weight and criteria assessing technical approach, compliance with SOW, and architecture.
*   **Vendor Experience and Past Performance (Weight):** Scoring weight and criteria evaluating track record, references, and relevant past project delivery.
*   **Financial Commercial Pricing (Weight):** Scoring weight and evaluation model for pricing, total cost of ownership (TCO), and rate competitiveness.
*   **Management Approach and Compliance (Weight):** Scoring weight evaluating project management methodology, staffing qualifications, and contractual compliance.
*   **Vendor Candidate Name and Proposal Identifier:** Identification of bidding vendor, proposal reference number, and submission date.
*   **Technical and Commercial Score Breakdown:** Individual numerical scores assigned across technical, commercial, and past performance criteria.
*   **Total Weighted Score:** Consolidated total weighted evaluation score out of 100 points for each candidate.
*   **Comparative Analysis and Value Justification:** Narrative justifying why the winning proposal offers superior value, quality, or pricing.
*   **Contract Award Recommendation and Sign-off:** Formal evaluation committee recommendation identifying preferred vendor for contract execution.
