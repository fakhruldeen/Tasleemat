## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Procurement Management Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.09.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Procurement Management Plan** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A governance artifact defining contract types, evaluation rules, legal jurisdiction, and vendor management protocols.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures legally sound, cost-effective acquisitions of external goods and services required for project delivery.

---

### 3. When?
Developed during planning prior to issuing bid documents and enforced across all contracting activities.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Procurement Manager and Project Manager, approved by Legal Counsel and Project Sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Utilize agile vendor contracting models (e.g., dynamic scope, fixed capacity) for iterative digital projects.
*   Enforce multi-stage international competitive bidding (ICB) for major capital equipment and infrastructure.

---

### Alignment
Integrates with Procurement Strategy, SOW, Source Selection Criteria, Cost Baseline, and Project Schedule.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Procurement Management Plan**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Procurement Strategy and Project Integration:** Integration strategy coordinating contract acquisitions with overall project schedule and scope baselines.
*   **Procurement Timetable and Milestone Alignment:** Key procurement lifecycle milestones (RFP issuance, bid evaluation, contract award, delivery).
*   **Procurement Roles and Sign-off Authority:** Defined roles (Procurement Manager, Legal Counsel, Technical Evaluators) and contracting authority limits.
*   **Vendor Performance Metrics and SLAs:** Objective metrics and service level agreements used to measure vendor performance.
*   **Standard Contract Types Selection:** Authorized contract structures (Firm Fixed Price, Time and Materials, Cost-Plus) mapped to deliverable types.
*   **Pre-Qualified Vendor Selection Approach:** Approach for evaluating and maintaining pre-qualified vendor lists and source directories.
*   **Legal Jurisdiction, Dispute Terms, and Currency:** Governing commercial legal jurisdiction, dispute resolution protocols, and billing currency.
*   **Procurement Risk and Independent Estimates:** Independent Cost Estimate (ICE) requirements and vendor default mitigation strategies.
