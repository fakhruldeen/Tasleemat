### Summary description
**Instruction:** A brief description of the requirement or need. The description should be no more than one or two sentences.

---

### Priority
**Instruction:** A way of prioritizing or ranking the requirements. This can be in summary groups, such as high, medium and low, or it can be numbered 1, 2, 3.

---

### Story
**Instruction:** This field can either be a user story that is prioritized, or it can indicate the name of a user story that is recorded elsewhere.

---

### Status
**Instruction:** Indicates if the requirement is not started, in progress, or complete.
