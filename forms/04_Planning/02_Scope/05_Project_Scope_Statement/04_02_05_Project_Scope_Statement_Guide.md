## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Project Scope Statement

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.02.05`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Project Scope Statement** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal detailed narrative establishing the exact boundary and deliverables of the project.

---

### 2. Why?
Builds shared understanding among stakeholders, prevents scope creep, and guides project team decisions.

---

### 3. When?
Developed in planning after requirements elicitation, baseline approved prior to major execution.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Project Manager and Business Analyst, signed off by Project Sponsor and Client.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Include vision and boundary statements for agile releases while decomposing detailed user stories in backlogs.
*   Incorporate strict contractual specs for third-party fixed-price delivery agreements.

---

### Alignment
Forms the core component of the Scope Baseline alongside the WBS and WBS Dictionary.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Project Scope Statement**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Detailed Project Scope Description:** Comprehensive narrative of the project work, characteristics of the end solution, and boundaries.
*   **Major Project Objectives and Outcomes:** Target business and technical outcomes directly addressed by executing this scope.
*   **Core Product Deliverables:** The primary end-products, technical systems, or physical deliverables generated by the project.
*   **Project Management and Governance Deliverables:** Management plans, status reports, audit logs, and compliance documentation delivered.
*   **Measurable Acceptance Criteria:** Specific, objective, and testable quality conditions required before deliverable formal acceptance.
*   **Acceptance Authority and Process:** Designated stakeholder authority and formal inspection workflow required for sign-off.
*   **Explicit Out-of-Scope Items:** Explicitly documented features, services, or activities that are intentionally excluded from project scope.
*   **Boundaries and Excluded Support:** Operational boundaries clarifying responsibilities not assumed by the project team.
*   **Identified Project Constraints:** Imposed boundaries including hard deadlines, budget caps, regulatory compliance, and technology limits.
*   **Identified Project Assumptions:** Critical planning assumptions regarding resource availability, vendor performance, and dependencies.
