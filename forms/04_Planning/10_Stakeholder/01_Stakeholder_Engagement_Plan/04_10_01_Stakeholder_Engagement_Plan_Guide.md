## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Stakeholder Engagement Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.10.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Stakeholder Engagement Plan** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A governance artifact detailing engagement assessment matrices (C vs. D), inter-stakeholder dynamics, and targeted engagement protocols.

---

### 2. Why?
Builds productive stakeholder partnerships, secures buy-in, resolves resistance, and sustains active project support.

---

### 3. When?
Developed during planning after stakeholder identification and updated as stakeholder dynamics evolve.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Project Manager and Change Lead, approved by Project Sponsor.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Integrate transparent sprint showcases and open backlog refinement for agile stakeholder collaboration.
*   Maintain highly confidential stakeholder management protocols for politically sensitive public transformations.

---

### Alignment
Integrates with Stakeholder Register, Communications Management Plan, Change Management Plan, and Project Charter.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Stakeholder Engagement Plan**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Stakeholder Name and Role:** Designated key stakeholder, organizational title, and sphere of influence.
*   **Current vs Desired Engagement Level:** Assessment of current engagement (Unaware, Resistant, Neutral, Supportive, Leading) vs. desired target level.
*   **Engagement Gap Analysis:** Analytical evaluation explaining the gap between current and desired engagement states.
*   **Inter-Stakeholder Dynamics and Coalitions:** Analysis of political dynamics, alliances, and influence networks among stakeholder groups.
*   **Stakeholder Expectations and Key Concerns:** Documented primary expectations, operational concerns, and sensitivity triggers.
*   **Tailored Engagement Strategy:** Specific actionable interventions designed to move the stakeholder to the desired engagement state.
*   **Engagement Monitoring and Feedback Loop:** Process for evaluating engagement effectiveness and adjusting tactics throughout delivery.
