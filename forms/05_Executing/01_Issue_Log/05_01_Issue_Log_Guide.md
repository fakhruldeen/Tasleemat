## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Issue Log

**Document Reference:** `PMO-05.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Issue Log** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A centralized log documenting all emergent problems, conflicts, and obstacles affecting project execution.

---

### 2. Why?
Unresolved issues threaten project deadlines, budget baselines, and quality standards if not systematically addressed.

---

### 3. When?
Activated at project kickoff and updated continuously throughout executing and monitoring phases.

---

### 4. Who?
Managed by the Project Manager with active collaboration from team leads, contributors, and impacted stakeholders.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Escalate blocking issues immediately to the Sponsor or Change Control Board.
*   Assign single-point accountability for resolving each logged issue.

---

### Alignment
The Issue Log aligns with Risk Register, Change Log, Project Status Report, and Lessons Learned Register.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Issue Log**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **ID:** Unique identifier for the issue (e.g., ISS-01).
*   **Type:** Type or classification of the issue (e.g., Technical, Resource, Schedule).
*   **Source of Issue:** Origin or root trigger of the issue (e.g., Vendor delay, Requirement change).
*   **Issue Description:** Detailed explanation of the issue and current condition.
*   **Priority:** Urgency or priority level (e.g., Urgent, High, Medium, Low).
*   **Impact on Objectives:** Potential or realized impact on scope, schedule, cost, or quality.
*   **Impacted Stakeholders:** Stakeholders or user groups affected by this issue.
*   **Responsible Party:** The person or role assigned to resolve or track the issue.
*   **Status:** Current status of the issue (e.g., Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed).
*   **Resolution Date:** Target or actual date by which the issue was resolved.
*   **Final Resolution:** Summary of the corrective action taken to resolve the issue.
*   **Comments:** Additional notes, context, or escalation details.
