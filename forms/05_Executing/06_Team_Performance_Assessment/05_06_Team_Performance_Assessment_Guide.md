## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Team Performance Assessment

**Document Reference:** `PMO-05.06`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Team Performance Assessment** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A holistic assessment measuring technical outputs, interpersonal dynamics, and development opportunities.

---

### 2. Why?
Improves teamwork, addresses competencies gaps early, and boosts team engagement and productivity.

---

### 3. When?
Conducted periodically throughout project execution and following major phase deliveries.

---

### 4. Who?
Conducted by the Project Manager in consultation with team leads and functional managers.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Use assessments constructively to build tailored coaching and recognition plans.
*   Incorporate 360-degree feedback for balanced interpersonal evaluations.

---

### Alignment
The Team Performance Assessment aligns with Resource Management Plan, Team Charter, and Training Plan.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Team Performance Assessment**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Technical Performance:** Evaluation of the team's ability to achieve scope, quality, schedule, and cost goals.
*   **Interpersonal Competency:** Evaluation of communication, collaboration, conflict management, and decision making.
*   **Team Characteristics:** Assessment of overall team morale, cohesion, trust, and shared commitment.
*   **Team Strengths and Weaknesses:** Identification of critical technical/interpersonal strengths and operational weaknesses.
*   **Areas for Development:** Targeted skill acquisition, training programs, team building, and coaching plans.
