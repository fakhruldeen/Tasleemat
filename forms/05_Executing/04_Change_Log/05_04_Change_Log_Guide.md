## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Change Log

**Document Reference:** `PMO-05.04`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Change Log** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A master tracking ledger documenting change requests, review status, CCB approvals, and impact.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides full traceability and auditability for project changes and baseline amendments.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained continuously throughout the project lifecycle.

---

### 4. Who?
Maintained by the Project Manager with oversight from the Change Control Board (CCB).

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Track total cumulative cost and schedule variance resulting from all approved changes.
*   Communicate change dispositions promptly to affected project teams.

---

### Alignment
The Change Log aligns with Change Management Plan, Project Baselines, and Project Status Report.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Change Log**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **ID:** Unique identifier for the change request (e.g., CR-01).
*   **Category:** Change category (Scope, Schedule, Cost, Quality, Resource).
*   **Description:** Summary description of the proposed change.
*   **Requestor:** Name or department submitting the change request.
*   **Submission Date:** Date the change request was formally submitted.
*   **Status:** Current lifecycle state of the request (Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Rejected).
*   **Disposition:** Decision made by the Change Control Board (CCB).
*   **Cost/Schedule Impact:** Estimated financial and timeline impact of the change.
*   **Type (Mandatory/Discretionary):** Whether the change is legally mandatory or discretionary.
*   **Configurable Items Impacted:** Configuration items, baselines, or specifications affected.
