# CHANGE REQUEST - LLM GENERATION GUIDE

> **System Prompt / Instructions:**
> This document serves as the detailed instruction set for generating the `CHANGE REQUEST`. When asked to populate this form, use the guidance provided for each section below to accurately generate the required content. Reference `parameters.md` for global project variables.

---

### Requestor
**Instruction:** The name, and if appropriate, the position of the person requesting the change

---

### Category
**Instruction:** Check a box to indicate the category of change.

---

### Description of change
**Instruction:** Describe the proposed change in enough detail to clearly communicate all aspects of the change.

---

### Justification for proposed change
**Instruction:** Indicate the reason for the change.

---

### Quality
**Instruction:** Describe the impact of the proposed change on the project or product quality.

---

### Requirements
**Instruction:** Describe the impact of the proposed change on the project or product requirements.

---

### Cost
**Instruction:** Describe the impact of the proposed change on the project budget, cost estimates, or funding requirements.

---

### Schedule
**Instruction:** Describe the impact of the proposed change on the schedule and whether it will change the critical path.

---

### Project
**Instruction:** documents Describe the impact of the proposed change on each project document.

---

### Comments
**Instruction:** Provide any comments that will clarify information about the requested change.
