## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Quality Audit Report

**Document Reference:** `PMO-05.05`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Quality Audit Report** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal audit report evaluating compliance with defined quality standards and process efficiency.

---

### 2. Why?
Identifies process inefficiencies, non-conformance risks, and opportunities to enhance deliverable quality.

---

### 3. When?
Executed at planned quality milestones during the execution phase.

---

### 4. Who?
Conducted by an independent Quality Auditor or PMO Quality Specialist and delivered to the Project Manager.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Focus audits on critical deliverables and high-risk process steps.
*   Track non-conformance items until corrective actions are fully verified.

---

### Alignment
The Quality Audit Report aligns with Quality Management Plan, Quality Metrics, and Corrective Action Plans.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Quality Audit Report**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Area Audited:** Specific processes, documentation, deliverables, or quality procedures evaluated.
*   **Good Practices from Similar Projects:** High-performing practices, techniques, and benchmarks identified during audit.
*   **Areas for Improvement:** Gaps, performance deviations, and recommended procedural optimizations.
*   **Deficiencies or Defects:** Recorded defects, non-compliance items, and assigned corrective actions.
*   **Implementation Tracking of Approved Changes:** Status of previously approved corrective actions, preventive actions, and defect repairs.
*   **Information to Share with Other Projects:** Valuable organizational insights, lessons, and quality benchmarks to disseminate.
*   **Comments:** General observations, auditor notes, and executive summary.
