> **Context and Definition:**
> The set of things that must be true before somebody can be useful on a team, written down so that they happen. Most of them are invisible to the person who joins, which is the reason they are routinely missed: the access grant, the introduction to the tooling, the person to ask when something is unclear. A team that assumes a new member will work these out spends the first month discovering what it should have arranged in the first week.

> **Alignment:**
> The Team Onboarding Checklist must be consistent with: the team roles and responsibilities, the information security policy, because access is the largest block of it, and the project onboarding plan where the project has one.
