# DECISION LOG - LLM GENERATION GUIDE

> **System Prompt / Instructions:**
> This document serves as the detailed instruction set for generating the log. When asked to populate this form, use the guidance provided for each section below to accurately generate the required content. Reference `parameters.md` for global project variables.

---

### ID
**Instruction:** Enter a unique decision identifier.

---

### Category
**Instruction:** Document the type of decision, such as technical, project, process, etc.

---

### Decision
**Instruction:** Provide a detailed description of the decision.

---

### Responsible party
**Instruction:** Identify the person authorized to make the decision.

---

### Priority
**Instruction:** Enter the date the decision was made and authorized.

---

### Comments
**Instruction:** Enter any further information to clarify the decision, alternatives considered, the reason the decision was made, and the impact of the decision.
