## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Executive Steering Committee Dashboard

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Executive Steering Committee Dashboard** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-06.13) establishing executive steering committee dashboard protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for executive steering committee dashboard.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Project Status Report (PMO-06.01), Earned Value Analysis (PMO-06.05)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Risk Report (PMO-04.08.06)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Decision Log (PMO-05.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Change Request (PMO-05.03)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Executive Steering Committee Dashboard**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Executive Summary and Overall Health RAG:** High-level synthesis of progress and overall project health color code (Red, Amber, Green).
*   **Baseline Variance Summary Schedule Cost Scope:** Quantitative variance snapshot across CPI, SPI, budget burn rate, and major milestones.
*   **Key Milestone Roadmap and Forecast Dates:** Target completion versus revised forecast for the next 3 to 6 strategic stage gates.
*   **Top Three Strategic Risks and Mitigations:** Highest-exposure threats with potential business impact and active contingency measures.
*   **Required Steering Committee Decisions and Guidance:** Explicit governance approvals, budget reallocations, or policy waivers requested from executive sponsors.
