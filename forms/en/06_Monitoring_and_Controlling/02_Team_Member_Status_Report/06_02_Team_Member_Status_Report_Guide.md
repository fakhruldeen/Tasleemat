## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Team Member Status Report

**Document Reference:** `PMO-06.02`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Team Member Status Report** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A structured status report completed by team members detailing personal deliverables, effort, quality, and blockers.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides granular visibility into work package progress and surfaces micro-level risks before they impact major milestones.

---

### 3. When?
Completed on a regular cadence (weekly or bi-weekly) during project executing and monitoring phases.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by individual project contributors or technical leads and submitted to the Project Manager or Team Lead.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Simplify or omit cost fields for internal resources where tracking is managed centrally.
*   Focus on blocker resolution and dependency alignment for Agile sprints.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Team Member Status Report**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Activities planned this reporting period:** Tasks and work packages scheduled for execution by this member during this period.
*   **Activities accomplished this reporting period:** Tasks and work packages successfully finalized by this member during this period.
*   **Activities planned but not accomplished this reporting period:** Scheduled tasks that were started but not completed or deferred.
*   **Root cause of schedule variances:** Underlying reasons for any task delays or workload obstacles.
*   **Funds planned to be spent this reporting period:** Planned labor hours or direct expenses allocated for the member this period.
*   **Funds spent this reporting period:** Actual labor hours or expenses incurred by the member during this period.
*   **Root cause of cost variances:** Reasons for discrepancies between planned and actual resource expenditures.
*   **Quality variances identified this period:** Defects, rework, or non-conformance issues encountered in deliverables.
*   **Planned corrective or preventive action:** Targeted remedial steps to correct quality issues or prevent recurring defects.
*   **Activities planned for next reporting period:** Upcoming work assignments and deliverables scheduled for the next interval.
*   **Costs planned for next reporting period:** Estimated hours or expenditures planned for the upcoming reporting period.
*   **New risks identified:** Potential technical, operational, or resource uncertainties identified by the member.
*   **New issues identified:** Immediate roadblocks, blockers, or dependencies requiring team resolution.
*   **Escalations:** Impediments requiring intervention from the Project Manager or Team Lead.
*   **Decisions made:** Technical or operational choices adopted by the team member during execution.
*   **Lessons learned and knowledge transfer:** Practical findings and best practices captured during task execution.
*   **Comments:** Additional reflections, notes, or contextual feedback from the team member.
