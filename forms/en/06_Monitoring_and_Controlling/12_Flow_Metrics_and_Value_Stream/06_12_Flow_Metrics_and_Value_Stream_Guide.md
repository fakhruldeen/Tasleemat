# Project Artifact: Flow Metrics and Value Stream Dashboard

**Document Reference:** `PMO-06.12`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Flow Metrics and Value Stream Dashboard** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework and PMI PMBOK standards.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Flow Metrics and Value Stream Dashboard**.
A formal modern delivery telemetry instrument tracking Lean/Agile flow metrics (Lead Time, Cycle Time, WIP limits, Flow Efficiency, and Throughput) across product value streams.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured flow metrics and value stream dashboard, strategic execution risks misalignment, lack of objective telemetry, and compromised governance.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed across iterative cycles.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by accountable governance authorities.

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Value Stream Parameters

*   **Value Stream Name and Definition:** The specific customer value stream, delivery pipeline, or product domain monitored by this flow dashboard.
*   **Measurement Window and Interval:** The operational reporting period, iteration window, and rolling sample interval evaluated for flow metrics.
*   **WIP Limits and Work Categorization:** Configured Work in Progress (WIP) caps across workflow stages and item type breakdown (Features, Defect Fixes, Technical Debt, and Risks).

#### Flow Telemetry and Metrics

*   **Lead Time and Cycle Time Percentiles:** 50th, 85th, and 95th percentile delivery times from work commitment to customer deployment, highlighting predictability.
*   **Flow Efficiency and Wait Time Ratio:** Percentage of total lead time spent in active value-adding touch work versus idle queue/blocking states.
*   **Throughput and Delivery Velocity:** Completed work items delivered per iteration cycle, indicating steady-state system capacity and throughput stability.

#### Flow Health and Bottleneck Remediation

*   **Flow Load and Work Age Health:** Inventory of active in-flight items, aging WIP items exceeding the 85th percentile threshold, and systemic work overload.
*   **Bottleneck Identification and Root Cause:** Specific workflow stage where work accumulates as queue inventory and the underlying process constraint.
*   **Kaizen Flow Optimization Action:** Targeted continuous improvement action, WIP constraint adjustment, or dependency unblocking intervention to improve flow.
