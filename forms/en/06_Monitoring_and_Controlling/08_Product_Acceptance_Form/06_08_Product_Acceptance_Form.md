## Product Acceptance Record

### ID
**Instruction:** Enter a unique requirement identifier from the requirements documentation.

### Requirement
**Instruction:** Enter the requirement description from the requirements documentation.

### Acceptance Criteria
**Instruction:** Enter the criteria for acceptance, stated so they can be judged as met or not met.

### Validation Method
**Instruction:** Describe the method of validating that the requirement meets the stakeholder's needs.

### Verification Method
**Instruction:** Optional. Record the verification method used to prove that the deliverable meets the requirement. Adding this column turns the form into a product verification, validation, and acceptance form.

### Status
**Instruction:** Document whether the requirement or deliverable was accepted or not.

### Sign-off
**Instruction:** Obtain the signature of the party accepting the product.

### Acceptance Date
**Instruction:** Enter the date the product was accepted. This dates the acceptance for the project record and for the requirements traceability matrix.

### Comments
**Instruction:** Add any comments relevant to the acceptance, such as conditions attached to the acceptance, or items deferred to a later acceptance.
