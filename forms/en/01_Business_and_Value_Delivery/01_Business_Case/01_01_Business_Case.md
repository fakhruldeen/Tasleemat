# BUSINESS CASE - LLM GENERATION GUIDE

> **System Prompt / Instructions:**
> This document serves as the detailed instruction set for generating the
> `BUSINESS CASE`. When asked to populate this form, use the guidance provided
> for each section below to accurately generate the required content.
> Reference `parameters.md` for global project variables.

> **Context & Definition:**
> A business case is the document that asks a decision-maker to release money. It is therefore not a description of a proposal but an argument that the proposal is better than the alternatives, including the alternative of doing nothing, which is always available and never written down.

The four sections follow the order in which a reader forms a judgement. The need establishes that there is something to solve and that it is worth solving now. The analysis establishes what is actually true today and what would happen if nothing changed. The options establish what could be done and what each costs over its whole life. The evaluation establishes how anyone will later tell whether the decision was right, and who is being asked to make the decision now.

## 1. Business Need

### Need Statement
**Instruction:** The problem or opportunity in one sentence, stated so that a reader who has never met the organisation can tell whether it has been solved. A need written as a goal cannot be falsified, and an unfalsifiable need is the reason a project that delivered everything it promised can still be judged a failure.

### Why Now
