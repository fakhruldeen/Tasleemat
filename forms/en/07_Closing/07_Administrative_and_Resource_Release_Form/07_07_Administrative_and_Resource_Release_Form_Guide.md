## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Administrative and Resource Release Form

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Administrative and Resource Release Form** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-07.07) establishing administrative and resource release form protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for administrative and resource release form.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Project or Phase Closeout (PMO-07.03), Contract Closeout Report (PMO-07.02)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Knowledge Transfer and Asset Archival Signoff (PMO-07.06)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Lessons Learned Summary (PMO-07.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Post-Implementation Review (PMO-07.05)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Administrative and Resource Release Form**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Resource Release Scope and Demobilization Schedule:** Staff members, contractors, and specialized consultants slated for scheduled release back to functional pools.
*   **Equipment Hardware and Physical Asset Return Log:** Inventory of laptops, test devices, security tokens, and field equipment inspected and returned.
*   **IT Access Revocation and Account Deprovisioning:** Revocation of VPN credentials, code repository commit access, cloud IAM roles, and facility badge access.
*   **Financial Cost Center Closure and Invoice Clearance:** Reconciliation of expense reports, settlement of pending vendor invoices, and freezing project cost centers.
*   **HR and Functional Manager Demobilization Signoff:** Joint sign-off between Project Manager, HR, and Functional Manager releasing resource obligations.
