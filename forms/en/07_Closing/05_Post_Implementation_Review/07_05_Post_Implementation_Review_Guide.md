# Project Artifact: Post-Implementation Review Report

**Document Reference:** `PMO-07.05`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Post-Implementation Review Report** in alignment with
the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A formal Tasleemat-aligned project artifact known as the **Post-Implementation Review Report**.
A formal closing and benefits realization audit document conducted 3 to 6 months after project handover to assess sustained value realization, operational adoption, and long-term organizational impacts.

---

### 2. Why?
Because without a structured post-implementation review report, decisions risk ambiguity and lack objective backing.

---

### 3. When?
This artifact is prepared during project lifecycle activities and refreshed as required.

---

### 4. Who?
**Responsibilities:** Prepared by the designated project lead and approved by key governance authorities.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Scale the depth of analysis to match project complexity and risk.
*   Ensure all key stakeholders are aligned before final sign-off.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Enterprise Strategic Plan
*   **Optional / Contextual:**
    *   Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:**
    *   Project Governance and Reporting Records

---

### 5. How?
Fill out each section carefully following the specific field guidance provided below:

#### Review Context

*   **Review Purpose and Evaluation Period:** The post-handover evaluation objectives and the operating timeframe assessed (typically 3 to 6 months post go-live).
*   **Lead Evaluator and Date:** The designated business assurance lead or PMO evaluator conducting the review and the completion date.

#### Benefits Realization Assessment

*   **Planned vs Realized Benefits:** Quantitative comparison of financial and operational benefits realized in steady-state operations versus business case projections.
*   **Operational Adoption Rate:** Metrics reflecting user adoption, system throughput, and behavioral integration into routine operations.

#### Operational Transition and Stability

*   **Operational Stability and Support:** Assessment of post-transition SLA adherence, support ticket volume, and operational maintenance handover efficacy.
*   **Outstanding Issues and Technical Debt:** Inventory of lingering operational bugs, unresolved defects, or technical debt requiring long-term maintenance.

#### Long-Term Insights and Recommendations

*   **Strategic Lessons Learned:** High-level strategic insights on solution viability, change management effectiveness, and business model impact.
*   **Ongoing Optimization Recommendations:** Prescriptive guidance for continuous optimization, future phase enhancements, and long-term capability extension.
