## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Risk Register

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.08.02`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Risk Register** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A master log recording risk IDs, descriptions, triggers, probabilities, impacts, exposure, and response actions.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides a single source of truth for managing uncertainties and tracking mitigation actions across the project.

---

### 3. When?
Initiated in planning and continuously updated throughout project execution and closing.

---

### 4. Who?
Maintained by Risk Manager and Project Manager, actions executed by Risk Owners.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Integrate as a prioritized risk backlog within agile standups and sprint planning.
*   Include quantitative monetary impact valuations for formal commercial contracts.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Risk Register**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Risk Identifier and Category:** Unique risk tracking code (e.g., RSK-01) and assigned RBS category.
*   **Risk Event Narrative and Root Cause:** Clear risk statement describing event cause, uncertain occurrence, and potential outcome.
*   **Risk Trigger and Symptoms:** Observable early warning signs and conditions indicating risk materialization.
*   **Assessed Probability and Impact Scores:** Quantitative probability rating and multidimensional impact score.
*   **Calculated Risk Exposure Rating:** Composite risk severity score (Probability x Impact) and overall priority ranking.
*   **Risk Response Strategy:** Selected treatment strategy (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, Exploit, Enhance, Share).
*   **Action Plan and Contingency Plan:** Specific preventive mitigation steps and secondary fallback contingency plan.
*   **Assigned Risk Owner and Status:** Designated individual accountable for managing the risk and current lifecycle status.
