## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Duration Estimates

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.03.06`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Duration Estimates** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A structured schedule table recording activity duration estimates, effort hours, estimation techniques, and contingency reserves.

---

### 2. Why?
Provides the mathematical time inputs required to build a realistic, achievable project schedule baseline.

---

### 3. When?
Calculated during schedule planning prior to network forward/backward pass schedule generation.

---

### 4. Who?
Created by Task Leads and Estimators, reviewed and consolidated by Project Scheduler.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Estimate in relative story points and velocity-based sprint horizons for agile initiatives.
*   Apply three-point statistical distributions for high-uncertainty research and development tasks.

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Duration Estimates**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Activity Identifier and Description:** The activity code and concise work summary for each estimated activity.
*   **Estimation Method Applied:** The specific technique used (analogous, parametric, three-point, or bottom-up).
*   **Estimated Effort Hours and Assigned Resources:** Total calculated person-hours of effort and assigned resource quantities.
*   **Estimated Duration:** Calculated working duration in work days or weeks required to complete the activity.
*   **Historical Data and Productivity Rates:** Empirical reference data, productivity metrics, and historical benchmarks used.
*   **Key Estimating Assumptions:** Foundational assumptions regarding resource availability, skill proficiencies, and work shifts.
*   **Schedule Contingency Reserve:** Calculated contingency buffer allocated to accommodate identified activity risks.
*   **Total Activity Duration with Contingency:** Final baseline duration inclusive of contingency buffer used in the schedule model.
