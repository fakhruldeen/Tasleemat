## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Quality Management Plan

**Document Reference:** `PMO-04.05.01`

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand
the purpose and effective usage of the **Quality Management Plan** in alignment with the
Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A governance artifact establishing QA/QC processes, defect thresholds, quality roles, and review gates.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures project deliverables meet customer standards, minimizes rework costs, and verifies regulatory compliance.

---

### 3. When?
Established during planning and executed continuously throughout execution, testing, and controlling.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by Quality Lead and Project Manager, approved by Project Sponsor and PMO.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   Incorporate automated CI/CD testing pipelines and acceptance test-driven development (ATDD) for agile software.
*   Enforce formal independent laboratory testing and material certifications for physical engineering assets.

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Quality Management Plan**, the responsible party
must populate the following critical sections based on the project context (ensure `parameters.md` is referenced for global project variables):

*   **Organizational Quality Policy and Framework:** The overarching corporate quality principles and methodology (e.g., ISO, Lean Six Sigma, CMMI).
*   **Applicable Industry Quality Standards:** Specific external regulatory, engineering, or legal quality standards enforced.
*   **Project Quality Goals and Thresholds:** Measurable quality targets regarding defect containment, test coverage, and customer satisfaction.
*   **Continuous Improvement Objectives:** Process enhancement goals and lessons learned integration mechanisms.
*   **Quality Assurance (QA) Process:** Audits and process compliance activities ensuring project execution adheres to approved standards.
*   **Quality Control (QC) Testing Protocol:** Inspection, automated testing, and peer review workflows used to verify deliverable quality.
*   **Non-Conformance and Defect Management:** Standard procedure for logging, prioritizing, resolving, and verifying non-conformance issues.
*   **Quality Roles and Responsibilities:** Designated quality assurance leads, test engineers, and peer review inspectors.
*   **Quality Review Gates and Sign-off Criteria:** Mandatory quality milestones and gate reviews required before client acceptance.
