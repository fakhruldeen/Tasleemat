## Tasleemat Forms Guide
# Project Artifact: Communications Distribution Matrix

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Communications Distribution Matrix** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-04.07.02) establishing communications distribution matrix protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for communications distribution matrix.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Communications Management Plan (PMO-04.07.01), Stakeholder Register (PMO-03.04)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Project Charter (PMO-03.01)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Project Status Report (PMO-06.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Meeting Minutes (PMO-05.11)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Communications Distribution Matrix**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Information Item and Description:** Specific reporting artifact, dashboard, or operational update item being distributed.
*   **Target Audience and Stakeholder Tier:** Designated recipient groups, stakeholder classification, and information consumption level.
*   **Distribution Channel and Delivery Format:** Approved transmission medium (email, portal, automated webhook, executive briefing) and file format.
*   **Cadence Trigger and Schedule:** Recurrence frequency (weekly, monthly, sprint boundary) or specific milestone event triggers.
*   **Responsible Sender and Author:** Designated role or individual accountable for preparing, validating, and sending the update.
*   **Confidentiality Classification and Access Controls:** Data sensitivity level (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) and access control policies.
