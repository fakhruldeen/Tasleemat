# Project Artifact: Information Security and Compliance Plan

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Information Security and Compliance Plan** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-04.13.01) establishing information security and compliance plan protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for information security and compliance plan.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Project Charter (PMO-03.01), Risk Management Plan (PMO-04.08.01)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Data Privacy and Ethics Assessment (PMO-02.06)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Deliverable Technical Inspection Checklist (PMO-05.15)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Transition to Operations Checklist (PMO-07.04)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Information Security and Compliance Plan**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Regulatory Framework and Applicable Standards:** Mandatory national regulations (NCA ECC, NDMO, GDPR, ISO 27001) governing project delivery.
*   **Data Classification and Privacy Tiering:** Information classification scheme (Public, Internal, Confidential, Highly Confidential) and PII protections.
*   **Technical and Administrative Security Controls:** Encryption at rest/in transit, identity and access management (IAM), multi-factor authentication, and audit trails.
*   **Security Assessment and Penetration Testing Cadence:** Vulnerability scanning, automated code analysis (SAST/DAST), and third-party penetration testing gateways.
*   **Incident Response Protocol and Breach Notification:** Escalation procedure, root cause containment, and mandatory regulatory reporting timeframes.
*   **Compliance Audit Gateways and Signoff Criteria:** Stage-gate evidence packages required by Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and legal counsel.
