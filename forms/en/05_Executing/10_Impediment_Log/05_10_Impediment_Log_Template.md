Context and Definition:
An impediment log is the record of the things standing between a team and the
work it has taken on, recorded so that they are raised rather than worked
around. An impediment is not an issue: an issue is something wrong inside the
team's own work, which the team fixes by working differently, while an
impediment is something the team cannot remove by itself. That distinction is
what the log records, and it is why every entry has an owner, because the
person raising an impediment is rarely the person who can clear it.

It can receive information from:
*   Sprint planning log
*   Daily stand-up notes
*   Team performance assessment

It provides information to:
*   Issue log, once the impediment becomes work the team can do
*   Decision log, when clearing it needed a decision
*   Project status report, because a long-standing impediment is a status fact
*   Retrospective, which is where a resolved one is worth reading again

Tailoring Tips:
*   Raise it rather than absorb it. A team that quietly works around an
    impediment looks productive for exactly as long as the workaround holds.
*   Name the cost, not the annoyance. An entry that says the work is blocked
    without saying what it costs cannot be weighed against anything else.
*   Write the resolution. The entry is closed when the impediment is gone, and
    the note about how it went is the part the next team can use.

Alignment:
The impediment log should be aligned and consistent with:
*   Issue log
*   Decision log
*   Risk register, because an impediment that recurs is a risk being realised

Section Instructions:
*   Impediment Log: for each impediment standing between the team and its
    work, give its identifier, the date it was raised, what it is in terms
    someone outside the team would understand, what it costs, who is pursuing
    it, and whether it is open, in progress or resolved.
