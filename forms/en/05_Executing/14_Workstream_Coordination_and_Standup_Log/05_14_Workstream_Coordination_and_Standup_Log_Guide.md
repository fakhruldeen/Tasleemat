# Project Artifact: Workstream Coordination and Standup Log

**Document Reference:** 

This document provides a comprehensive, professional reference to understand the purpose and effective usage of the **Workstream Coordination and Standup Log** in alignment with the Tasleemat framework.

---

### 1. What?
A standardized PMO deliverable (PMO-05.14) establishing workstream coordination and standup log protocols and governance baselines.

---

### 2. Why?
Ensures rigorous operational alignment, transparent stakeholder visibility, and structured project governance for workstream coordination and standup log.

---

### 3. When?
Maintained throughout project execution and updated during scheduled reviews or milestone transitions.

---

### 4. Who?
Authored by the Project Manager or designated lead; reviewed and approved by Project Sponsors and Governance Committees.

---

### Tailoring Tips
*   For small/agile teams, integrate into lightweight digital boards or automated reporting webhooks.
*   For large/enterprise programs, mandate formal document control and signed executive stage-gate signoffs.

---

### Alignment & Dependencies

#### 1. Pre-requisites & Inputs (Upstream Dependencies)
*   **Mandatory:** Sprint Planning Log (PMO-04.03.10), Team Charter (PMO-04.06.05)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Impediment Log (PMO-05.10)

#### 2. Downstream Dependents
*   **Mandatory:** Action Items Log (PMO-05.13)
*   **Optional / Contextual:** Flow Metrics and Value Stream (PMO-06.12)

---

### 5. How?
To accurately and professionally complete the **Workstream Coordination and Standup Log**, populate all required sections based on project context:

*   **Coordination Session Date and Attendees:** Meeting timestamp, participating workstream leads, Scrum Masters, and engineering facilitators.
*   **Accomplished Items since Previous Sync:** Key deliverable increments, completed user stories, or milestones achieved across streams.
*   **Planned Focus for Current Cycle:** Immediate priority work packages and sprint goals committed for the next 24-48 hours.
*   **Cross Stream Dependencies and Handoffs:** Critical interface handoffs, API integrations, or hardware prerequisites between teams.
*   **Active Impediments and Escalation Owners:** Blockers requiring Scrum of Scrums intervention or PMO escalation with assigned lead.
