## 1. Executive Summary & Business Need
* **Need Statement:** The contact center handles 4.2 million customer interactions annually with an average wait time of 4.2 minutes, resulting in an annual operating expense of 28,000,000 SAR and a customer satisfaction (CSAT) rating dropping to 71%.
* **Why Now:** Surge in digital-first mobile users and the enforcement of the new Communications and Information Technology Commission (CITC) customer care SLA mandates.
* **Cost of Inaction:** Losing 3.5% of postpaid subscriber base annually to competitors, representing 42,000,000 SAR in unrecoverable lost recurring revenue over 3 years.
