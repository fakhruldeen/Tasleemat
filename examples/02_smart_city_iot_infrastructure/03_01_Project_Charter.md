## 1. Executive Mandate & Strategic Purpose
The Metropolitan Environmental Sensor Project deploys **450 IoT sensor stations** and **60 LoRaWAN Gateway Towers** across the metropolitan area to monitor air quality ($PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}, NO_2, SO_2$), micro-climate temperatures, acoustic noise, and flash-flood water drainage levels in real time.

* **Strategic Alignment:** Direct contribution to National Urban Quality of Life Index and Municipal Digital Twin program.
* **Budget Envelope:** **32,000,000 SAR** fixed-price EPC contractual ceiling.
* **Execution Period:** 18 Calendar Months (2026-02-01 to 2027-07-31).

---

## 2. Work Breakdown Structure & Primary Deliverables
1. **Civil Works & Mast Foundations:** 60 reinforced concrete tower pads with solar-battery backup systems.
2. **Sensor Hardware Manufacturing & Testing:** Calibration and field ruggedization (IP67, $55^\circ	ext{C}$ operating temperature tolerance).
3. **LoRaWAN & Cellular Network Backbone:** Low-latency encrypted wireless telemetry transmission.
4. **Municipal Command & Control Platform:** Cloud data lake ingestion with real-time alerting GIS dashboards.
