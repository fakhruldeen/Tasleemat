## 1. Scope of Engineering & Installation Services
The Contractor shall deliver turnkey EPC services for **450 Multi-Sensor Ambient Stations** and **60 LoRaWAN Gateway Base Stations**:
* **Foundation & Civil Construction:** Soil bearing capacity testing, excavation, reinforced concrete pad casting ($1.8\text{m} \times 1.8\text{m} \times 0.6\text{m}$), and solar mast erection.
* **Sensor Station Assembly:** Mounting calibrated particulate matter ($PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}$), gas sensors ($NO_2, SO_2, CO$), acoustic decibel meters, and solar-PV lithium storage modules.
* **Telemetry Integration:** Establishing encrypted MQTT communication protocols with the Municipal Central Data Lake over 5G/LoRaWAN dual fallback links.

---

## 2. Technical Standards & Testing Acceptance
1. **Environmental Ruggedization:** All field equipment must achieve **IP67 ingress protection** and continuous operation between $-5^\circ\text{C}$ and $+55^\circ\text{C}$.
2. **Sensor Calibration:** Multi-point gas chamber calibration with certified NIST traceable reference gases.
3. **Data Availability SLA:** $\ge 99.85\%$ verified monthly data packet delivery uptime.
